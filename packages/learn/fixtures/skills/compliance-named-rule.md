Compliance findings must cite the specific rule, statute, or framework
clause being addressed (e.g., "12 CFR Part 1024.41 loss mitigation",
"SOC 2 CC6.1 logical access"). Compliance buyers do not buy from
vendors who don't know the rule numbers. If the evidence doesn't pin a
specific rule, mark `fit` low.

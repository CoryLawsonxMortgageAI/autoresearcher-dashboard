when: a finding doesn't name a specific buyer or a specific budget owner
verticals: []
priority: 5
---

Refuse to emit any finding whose evidence does not name at least one
concrete buyer (a person with a title, or a specific company department
with budget authority). "SMBs are frustrated with X" is not a buyer.
"Loan officers at IMBs with $50M+ originations" is. If you cannot find
a named-buyer signal in the evidence, log it and skip — do not
hallucinate a buyer to fill the slot.

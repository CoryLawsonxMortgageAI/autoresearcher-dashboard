For mortgage findings, frame the wedge in terms of cycle-time impact
(days-to-close, conditions-cleared-per-LO-week) or compliance-load
impact (audit findings closed, exam-prep hours), not "AI" generally.
A loan officer reading the digest cares about closing more loans this
month, not about model architectures.
